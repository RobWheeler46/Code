Support Us

Help us keep giving young people skills for life

Everything we do runs on the generosity of volunteers, families and our local community. There are lots of ways to help 7th Swindon, whether you have time to give, a skill to share, some kit to pass on, or a little to donate. Every bit makes a real difference to young people in North Swindon.

Ways you can help

  • 🙌

    Volunteer your time

    The biggest gift you can give is a few hours. Every role comes with full training and a warm welcome, and many are flexible, so you can help as often or as little as suits you.

    See how to volunteer →
  • 💜

    Make a donation

    Donations help us keep weekly subscriptions low, buy and repair equipment, and make sure cost is never a barrier for a young person. Get in touch and we will tell you the best way to give.

    Get in touch about donating →
  • 🎉

    Fundraise for us

    An event, a sponsored challenge or a collection at work can raise a surprising amount, and it is good fun too. We are always happy to help you set something up.

    Tell us your idea →
  • 🎒

    Give equipment

    Good quality camping and activity kit, tents, or uniform in good condition can find a second life with us. Please ask us what we need before dropping anything off.

    Ask what we need →
  • 🧰

    Offer a skill or business support

    From a practical trade to sponsorship, raffle prizes or printing, local businesses and individuals can support us in kind. If you have something to offer, we would love to hear from you.

    Talk to us →
  • 📣

    Spread the word

    Following and sharing our updates helps more families find us and more volunteers step forward. It costs nothing and it really does help.

    Follow us on Instagram →
Gift Aid. If you pay tax in the UK, Gift Aid lets us claim an extra 25 pence for every pound you donate, at no cost to you. Just ask us for a Gift Aid form when you give.

Talk to us about supporting us

Not sure where to start? Get in touch and choose “Supporting us” on our contact form, or email glv@7thswindon.org.uk. We would be glad to help you find the right way to get involved.
