Contact us

Choose a reason and your message goes straight to the right team. We are a group of volunteers and will get back to you as soon as we can.

Already part of 7th Swindon? You will find member info, notices and documents in 7thPortal. You can still send a message below if you need to.

We will only use your details to respond to your enquiry.

Where your message goes

Pick a reason at the top of the form and we send your message to the right volunteers, so you reach the people who can help fastest.
