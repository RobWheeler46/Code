      Good to know

      Join our waiting list through Online Scout Manager (OSM). A place isn't a guaranteed start date, it depends on local availability and a chat with the team.

      Part of Swindon North Scout District, we're one of the largest Scout Groups in North Swindon, helping over 300 young people develop skills for life every week.
