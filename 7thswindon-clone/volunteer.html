Volunteer With Us

Scouting only happens because of brilliant volunteers, and there's a place for you, whatever your skills or spare time. Not every role means being there every week. Answer three quick questions and we'll suggest ways you could help.

  1. 1How to help
  2. 2Availability
  3. 3Opportunities
  4. 4Interested

How would you like to help?

Pick what appeals most, you can change your mind later.

What is your availability?

Select anything that suits, this just helps us suggest a good fit. Optional.

Interested? Tell us a little about yourself

You are interested in: General interest

What happens next: an informal, no-pressure chat, then we explore the role together, then if it is a fit, the official joining steps (including the checks and training every volunteer completes). No commitment at this stage.

🔒 We will only use your details to talk to you about volunteering.
