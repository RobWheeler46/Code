Accessibility

Making this website work for everyone

Last updated 17 November 2026.

We want everyone to be able to use our website, whatever device they use and however they browse. This page explains the standard we aim for, what we have done, where we know there is more to do, and how to tell us about a problem.

The standard we aim for

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.2 at level AA, which is the standard widely used across the United Kingdom. We build and test the site with this in mind, and we check our pages with automated accessibility tools as we make changes. Automated testing does not catch everything, so if something is hard to use, please tell us.

What we have done

  • •A skip link at the top of every page so you can jump straight to the main content.
  • •Full keyboard support, with a clear outline showing where you are on the page.
  • •Colour choices checked for contrast, so text stays readable against its background.
  • •Meaningful headings and labels, and descriptive text for images.
  • •A layout that adapts to phones, tablets and desktops, and works when you zoom in.
  • •Respect for the reduce motion setting, so animations calm down if you have asked your device to limit them.

Where we know there is more to do

We are honest about the parts that may not be perfect:

  • •Some content comes from other services, such as embedded videos and the spam check on our forms. These bring their own accessibility behaviour, which we do not fully control.
  • •Some documents we link to, for example brand guidelines and meeting papers, are created by others and may not be fully accessible. If you need one in a different format, please ask and we will help.

Tell us about a problem

If you find something on this website that is hard to use, or you need information in a different format such as large print or plain text, please get in touch. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will do our best to put it right and to get the information to you another way.

Contact us

If you are not happy with our response

We will always try to help. If you are not satisfied with how we have dealt with your request, please come back to us so we can look again. You can also find general advice about digital accessibility from the disability charity Scope.
