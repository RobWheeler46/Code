About 7th Swindon

One of North Swindon's largest Scout Groups

Every week we help over 300 young people aged 6 to 18 across North Swindon develop skills for life, making friends, trying new things and having adventures, supported by a brilliant team of volunteers.

Who we are

7th Swindon Scout Group runs Beavers, Cubs and Scouts across several North Swindon localities, and works in partnership with local Explorer units for young people aged 14 to 18. We're a community of young people, parents, carers and volunteers who believe every young person deserves the chance to discover what they're capable of.

Our sections meet in areas including Taw Hill, Tadpole Garden Village, Abbey Meads and Oakhurst. Use Find Your Section to see the meetings that fit your child.

How we're run

We're a charity led by a volunteer Trustee Board, who make sure the Group runs safely, sustainably and in line with The Scout Association's policies. We publish our AGM minutes and trustees' reports for transparency.
